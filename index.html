THE AKSHAYA GRAMA IDEA

More than a homestay.
A sense of belonging.

Akshaya Grama is a rural tourism experience that invites you to discover authentic village life, local food, farming, traditional culture, nature and the everyday occupations of the rural community.

Rather than being merely a place to stay, we connect guests with the people, livelihoods, traditions and surroundings of Chinnasandra through meaningful local experiences and interactions.
