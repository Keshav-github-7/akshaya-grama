The Akshaya Grama idea

More than a homestay.

Akshaya Grama is a rural tourism experience that offers visitors an opportunity to experience authentic village life, local food, farming, traditional culture, nature, and the everyday occupations of the rural community.

Rather than being merely a place to stay, Akshaya Grama connects guests with the people, livelihoods, traditions, and surroundings of the village through meaningful local experiences and interactions.

“Stay in a rural home + experience the life, livelihoods, culture and surroundings of the village.”
Chinnasandra VillageChintamani Taluk • Karnataka

Six ways to experience rural India

Choose your way into village life.

Our experiences are designed around the real people, occupations, food, fields and everyday rhythms of Chinnasandra.

01

Village Life

Meet local families, observe everyday activities, understand household practices and participate in simple village activities where appropriate.

02

Traditional & Local Food

Enjoy home-cooked food, learn traditional preparation methods and discover the ingredients and stories behind local dishes.

Don’t just eat rural food—understand its story.
03

Farming

Visit agricultural fields, understand local crops and cultivation, interact with farmers and participate in simple seasonal activities when feasible.

04

Village Walk

Take a guided walk through village streets, agricultural areas, water resources, community spaces and everyday activity.

05

Rural Livelihoods

Go behind the scenes of sericulture, dairy, poultry and cotton wick making. Meet local people and understand how livelihoods are created.

From local occupation to local livelihood.
06

Nearby Attractions & Heritage

Combine village life with nature, culture and heritage around Chinnasandra through curated local sightseeing.

Our signature experience

Meet the livelihoods behind the village.

These are not staged performances. Guests are introduced to real local activities and the people who carry them forward.

01

Sericulture

Mulberry cultivation, the silkworm lifecycle and the journey from silkworm to silk.

02

Cotton Wick Making

From cotton to a locally made product and livelihood—preparation, shaping, packing and supply.

03

Dairy

Cattle rearing, feeding, daily care and an introduction to milk production.

04

Poultry

Understand poultry farming, day-to-day operations and the work of local entrepreneurs.
